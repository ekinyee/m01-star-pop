隐私说明 / Privacy

本试玩版本无需登录，没有广告、第三方追踪或远程游戏统计。最高分仅保存在当前浏览器的 localStorage，可通过清除网站数据删除。

本地运行时，请求只发送到本机服务器。公开部署后，托管服务商可能按其政策处理 IP 地址和访问日志；这与游戏内统计不同。

This demo has no login, ads, third-party trackers, or remote gameplay analytics. Your best score is saved only in this browser's localStorage and can be removed by clearing site data. A public hosting provider may process IP addresses and access logs under its own policies.
