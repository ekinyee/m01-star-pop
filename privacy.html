Privacy

This demo has no login, ads, third-party trackers, or remote gameplay analytics. Your best score and chosen language are saved only in this browser's localStorage and can be removed by clearing site data.

When run locally, requests go only to your local server. A public hosting provider may process IP addresses and access logs under its own policies. This is separate from gameplay analytics.

Before adding ads or remote analytics, this notice will be updated with operator contact details and the relevant data practices.

隐私说明

本试玩版本无需登录，没有广告、第三方追踪或远程游戏统计。最高分和所选语言仅保存在当前浏览器的 localStorage，可通过清除网站数据删除。

本地运行时，请求只发送到本机服务器。公开部署后，托管服务商可能按其政策处理 IP 地址和访问日志；这与游戏内统计不同。

上线广告或远程统计前，将更新本说明并补充运营者联系方式。
